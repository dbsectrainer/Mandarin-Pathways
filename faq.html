常见问题Frequently asked questions

找不到答案？联系我们。Can't find your answer? Contact us.

真的免费吗？Is it really free?
是的，完全免费：全部40天课程、音频、测验、闪卡、写字和阅读练习。没有付费墙，没有订阅，没有广告。项目开源在 GitHub 上。Yes — completely. All 40 lessons, audio, quizzes, flashcards, writing, and reading practice. No paywall, no subscription, no ads. The project is open source on GitHub.
需要注册账户吗？Do I need an account?
不需要。打开网站就能学。您的进度保存在浏览器本地，而不是服务器上。No. Just open the site and start. Your progress is saved locally in your browser, not on a server.
故事学习法是怎么运作的？How does learning with stories work?
每天一课：先听母语音频并跟随同步高亮的文本阅读，然后做测验、复习闪卡、练写字。语法模式融入课文中自然吸收。详见学习方法指南。One lesson a day: listen to native audio while reading the synchronized text, then quiz yourself, review flashcards, and practice writing. Grammar is absorbed from the text rather than drilled. See the method guide for details.
我是零基础，从哪里开始？I'm a complete beginner — where do I start?
从第1天开始。第1-7天专讲拼音和声调，不需要任何基础。也可以先读拼音与声调入门。Start with Day 1. Days 1-7 cover pinyin and tones and assume nothing. The pinyin & tones guide is a good primer too.
我已经会一些中文，怎么办？I already know some Mandarin — what then?
做一下水平测试，它会推荐一个合适的起始天数，直接从那里开始。Take the placement test — it recommends a starting day, and you can jump in right there.
课程分哪五个阶段？What are the five course stages?
第1-7天拼音与发音；第8-14天基础日常用语；第15-22天文化背景与日常生活；第23-30天职业中文；第31-40天高级流利度。详见课程大纲。Days 1-7 pinyin & pronunciation; Days 8-14 essential daily phrases; Days 15-22 cultural context & daily life; Days 23-30 professional Mandarin; Days 31-40 advanced fluency. See the full syllabus.
每天需要多长时间？How long does each day take?
大约20-30分钟：听读课文约10分钟，测验5分钟，闪卡和写字5-10分钟。About 20-30 minutes: roughly 10 for listening and reading, 5 for the quiz, and 5-10 for flashcards and writing.
可以离线使用或安装吗？Can I use it offline or install it?
可以。本站是PWA应用：在手机或电脑浏览器中选择"添加到主屏幕/安装"，访问过的课程会缓存下来，离线也能学。Yes. The site is a PWA: choose "Add to Home Screen" / "Install" in your browser, and lessons you've visited are cached for offline use.
我的进度存在哪里？怎么备份？Where is my progress stored, and how do I back it up?
进度只保存在当前浏览器的本地存储中。在我的进度页面，点击页眉的备份按钮可以导出JSON文件，也可以在新设备上导入。清除浏览器数据会清除进度，请注意备份。Progress lives in this browser's local storage. On the My Progress page, the backup button in the header exports a JSON file you can import on another device. Clearing browser data clears progress, so back up first.
中文、拼音、英文三种模式有什么区别？What's the difference between the zh, Pinyin, and English modes?
同一节课的三种显示方式：简体中文模式只显示汉字；拼音模式显示注音和声调轮廓，配普通话音频；英文模式显示翻译，配英文讲解音频。建议按英文→拼音→中文的顺序逐步过渡。Three views of the same lesson: Chinese mode shows characters; Pinyin mode shows romanization with tone contours and Mandarin audio; English mode shows the translation with an English explanation track. A good progression is English → Pinyin → Chinese.
有口语和写作练习吗？Is there speaking and writing practice?
有。写作练习提供笔顺动画和手写画布（汉字、句子、翻译三种模式）；口语方面建议跟读每课音频（影子跟读法），拼音模式的声调轮廓会帮您校准发音。Yes. Writing practice offers stroke-order animations and a drawing canvas (characters, sentences, translation). For speaking, shadow each lesson's audio — the tone contours in Pinyin mode help you calibrate your pronunciation.
老师可以在课堂上使用吗？Can teachers use this in class?
非常欢迎。免费、无账户、不收集学生数据，还有可打印的课文。请看教师专区的课堂使用建议。Absolutely. It's free, account-less, collects no student data, and every lesson prints cleanly. See the For Teachers page for classroom ideas.
