拼音与声调入门Pinyin and tones for beginners

中文发音没有想象中难——它只是一个规则清晰的系统。这篇指南带你认识它。Mandarin pronunciation is less scary than it looks — it's a small, regular system. This guide walks you through it.

拼音是什么？What is pinyin?

拼音是用拉丁字母标注普通话发音的官方系统。它不是另一种文字，而是一张"发音地图"：每个汉字对应一个音节，每个音节由声母（开头的辅音）、韵母（后面的元音部分）和声调组成。Pinyin is the official system for writing Mandarin sounds with Latin letters. It isn't a separate writing system — it's a pronunciation map. Every character maps to one syllable, and every syllable is built from an initial (the opening consonant), a final (the vowel part), and a tone.

普通话只有大约400个基本音节——远少于英语。这意味着发音系统小到可以在一两周内熟悉。Mandarin has only about 400 basic syllables — far fewer than English. The whole sound system is small enough to get familiar with in a week or two.

四个声调（加轻声）The four tones (plus neutral)

声调决定意思：同一个音节"ma"，四个声调是四个不同的词。下面是同一音节在五种声调下的轮廓，图形显示音高如何变化：Tones change meaning: the same syllable "ma" is a different word in each tone. Below is the same syllable in all five tones — the glyphs show how the pitch moves:

mā（妈 mother）· má（麻 hemp）· mǎ（马 horse）· mà（骂 scold）· ma（吗 question particle）mā (mother) · má (hemp) · mǎ (horse) · mà (scold) · ma (question particle)

  • 一声（ā）：First tone (ā): 高而平，像唱歌时拖住一个音。high and flat, like holding a sung note.
  • 二声（á）：Second tone (á): 从中音升到高音，像疑问的"啊？"。rising from mid to high, like a questioning "huh?".
  • 三声（ǎ）：Third tone (ǎ): 先降后升，日常语速中常常只降不升。dips down then rises; at natural speed it often just stays low.
  • 四声（à）：Fourth tone (à): 从高音干脆地降下来，像下命令。a sharp fall from high, like giving a command.
  • 轻声（a）：Neutral tone (a): 短促、轻巧，跟着前一个音节走。short and light, riding on the syllable before it.

练声调的技巧Tips for training your tones

  • 两两成对练习（声调对），因为真实词语大多是双音节。Drill tones in pairs — most real words are two syllables, so tone pairs are what you'll actually say.
  • 夸张地模仿音频，宁可过头也不要含糊。Exaggerate when you shadow the audio; too much is better than too flat.
  • 在课程的拼音模式里打开声调轮廓，把耳朵听到的和眼睛看到的对上。Use Pinyin mode in the lessons — the tone contours let your eyes confirm what your ears hear.

常见发音误区Common pitfalls

  • q / x / j: 都比英语更靠前、更"尖"，舌尖抵下齿。all sharper and more forward than English — tongue tip behind the lower teeth.
  • zh / ch / sh vs z / c / s: 前者卷舌，后者平舌。慢慢对比着练。the first set curls the tongue back; the second keeps it flat. Practice them side by side, slowly.
  • ü: 嘴唇圆起来发"i"。在 ju/qu/xu 中虽然写作 u，读的仍是 ü。say "ee" with rounded lips. In ju/qu/xu it's spelled u but still pronounced ü.
  • 不要用英语重音代替声调——中文每个音节的声调都算数。Don't map English stress onto Mandarin — every syllable's tone counts.
