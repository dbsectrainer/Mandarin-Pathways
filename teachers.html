为什么故事适合课堂Why stories work in class

故事给语言提供了语境：学生不是在背孤立的词表，而是在有意义的场景中反复遇到同样的句型。配合母语音频和逐句高亮，同一份材料可以同时支持听力、朗读、跟读和讨论——不同水平的学生还能各自切换到中文、拼音或英文模式。Stories give language a context: instead of memorizing word lists, students meet the same patterns again and again inside meaningful situations. With native audio and phrase-by-phrase highlighting, one lesson supports listening, choral reading, shadowing, and discussion — and students at different levels can switch the same material between Chinese, Pinyin, and English modes.

课堂使用建议Classroom ideas

  • 热身听力：Warm-up listening: 上课先盲听当天音频一遍，请学生说出听到的词。play the day's audio once without text and have students call out words they caught.
  • 跟读齐读：Choral reading: 投影课文，跟着高亮逐句齐读，模仿声调。project the lesson and read along with the highlighting, imitating the tones together.
  • 测验做出门条：Quiz as exit ticket: 下课前让学生完成当天测验并报告分数。end class with the day's quiz and have students report their score.
  • 闪卡当作业：SRS as homework: 学生收藏本课短语，回家用间隔重复复习。students star the lesson's phrases and review them at home with spaced repetition.
  • 选修主题：Elective topics: 用五个补充主题（教育、爱好、情绪、天气、比较）做扩展课。use the five supplementary topics (education, hobbies, emotions, weather, comparisons) for enrichment lessons.
